Career Goals

Consulting

After graduating, I hope to work in consulting, where I can combine my computer science background with an understanding of business problems. I want to help clients identify their needs, document clear requirements, and choose practical technology solutions. I am also interested in using data to support recommendations and explain the reasoning behind them.

Business and Technology

My goal is to bridge the gap between clients and technical teams. I want to translate business needs into clear technical requirements and explain technical ideas in a way that people without a computer science background can understand. My interests in human-computer interaction and artificial intelligence motivate me to explore solutions that are useful, easy to use, and suited to the people using them.

Professional Growth

I want to continue developing my programming, data analysis, and AI skills through coursework and projects. Alongside these technical skills, I want to improve how I communicate with stakeholders and present recommendations. Gaining more practical experience will help me understand how technology can support everyday business decisions and prepare me for a consulting role.
